Appforce Pro

Appforce Pro delivers live TV, video on demand, catch-up and a programme guide with remote-friendly navigation, backed by Laravel APIs and built in React.

The problem

People with their own IPTV subscriptions need a player that works the same way on whatever screen they own, from a Fire TV Stick to a Samsung or LG smart TV, and that is easy to drive with a remote.

Appforce Pro is that player. It is a media player only and ships no channels or content of its own.

How it was built

  1. 01

    One player, many televisions

    Apps for Android TV and Fire TV, plus a React frontend for Samsung Tizen 4+, LG webOS 3+ and Electron desktop.

  2. 02

    Every playlist format

    MAG Stalker Portal, M3U, M3U8 and Xtream Codes, with a multi-format video engine covering HLS, MPEG-TS and AVPlay.

  3. 03

    Built for the remote

    Spatial navigation, an EPG TV guide, catch-up TV, video on demand and live TV, with QR-code playlist setup from a phone.

  4. 04

    Licensing behind the scenes

    A Laravel API and the AppForce Control Panel handle trials, licenses and resellers.

Where it is now

  • Available for Android TV, Fire TV, Samsung and LG smart TVs.
  • Sold with a three-day trial and a paid license, through resellers and direct.

Who worked on it
