BosonPOS

BosonPOS runs point of sale, stock and inventory across multiple branches in one system. Built with Laravel, Vue.js and Tailwind CSS.

The problem

Retailers with more than one branch often run sales on one tool, stock on a spreadsheet and supplier balances in a notebook. Every transfer between branches is a chance for the numbers to drift.

BosonPOS is our own point-of-sale and inventory system for that kind of business.

How it was built

  1. 01

    Stock that follows the goods

    Sales, purchases, stock transfers and adjustments across multiple locations, all on one ledger.

  2. 02

    The people around the counter

    Customer and supplier ledgers, areas and salesmen, staff salaries, and discounts.

  3. 03

    Payments people actually use

    Cash, card, bank transfer, JazzCash and Easypaisa.

  4. 04

    Reports from day to year

    Daily sales through to profit and expense breakdowns.
