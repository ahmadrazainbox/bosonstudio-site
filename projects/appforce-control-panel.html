AppForce Control Panel

The AppForce Control Panel manages devices, licenses and resellers across the AppForce applications. Built with Laravel, Vue.js and Stripe.

Client
DMN Technology GmbH (GloriaForce), Gütersloh, Germany
Sector
Operations
Year
2022 – present
Our role
Architecture and full build
Platforms
Web
Stack
Laravel · Vue.js · Stripe

The problem

Every device running an AppForce app needs a trial, a license, an expiry date and a reseller who sold it. Across several apps and tens of thousands of devices, that cannot live in spreadsheets.

The control panel is the backend behind Appforce Pro, Appforce Classic and the wider GloriaForce app range.

How it was built

  1. 01

    A reseller hierarchy

    Resellers, sub-resellers and retailers, each with their own portal and a credit-based licensing system.

  2. 02

    The whole device lifecycle

    Registration, trial and license status, expiry and remote blocking, with checksum validation and a registry of tampered devices.

  3. 03

    Safe updates

    Staged app and over-the-air updates with beta rollouts, role-based access, full activity logs, and 2FA with OTP verification.

  4. 04

    Lean by design

    Stripe payments, TMDB metadata for media, and a database kept under 50 MB despite the size of the user base.

Where it is now

  • Manages more than 70,000 Android devices across the AppForce apps.
  • Runs licensing and payments for the AppForce reseller network.
