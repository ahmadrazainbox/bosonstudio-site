10X Events

10X Events covers QR ticketing, real-time check-ins, Shopify and HubSpot integrations, and custom email and SMS. Built with Laravel, Vue.js and Quasar.

Sector
Events
Year
2022 – 2024
Our role
Built from scratch
Platforms
Web
Stack
Laravel · Vue.js · Quasar

The problem

Large live events sell tickets in one system, manage contacts in another and check people in on the day with a third. Every handoff slows the queue at the door.

How it was built

  1. 01

    Connected to the shop and the CRM

    Shopify and HubSpot integrations, so ticket sales and contacts stay in step.

  2. 02

    Fast at the door

    QR ticketing with real-time check-ins.

  3. 03

    Messages and roles

    Customisable email and SMS, multi-tenant accounts and dynamic role management.

  4. 04

    Tested under load

    Load testing with K6 before event days.
