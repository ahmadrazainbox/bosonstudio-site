Survey & Review Management

Mobile surveys, a web portal and NFC and badge scanning, with collected data available across the workflow. Built with ColdFusion, Ionic and Angular.

Sector
Research
Our role
Mobile app and web portal
Platforms
iOS and Android (Ionic), web
Stack
ColdFusion · Ionic · Angular

The problem

At trade shows and live events, every conversation at the stand is a potential lead, but paper forms and business cards get lost before anyone follows up.

How it was built

  1. 01

    Surveys on the stand

    An Ionic and Angular mobile app for running visitor surveys.

  2. 02

    Scan instead of type

    NFC and badge scanning to capture attendee details in a second.

  3. 03

    One portal for the data

    A web portal on ColdFusion and Firebase where collected data is available across the workflow.
