Hazrify

Hazrify handles attendance, leave, location monitoring and employee management for multiple employers. Built with Laravel, Ionic and Angular.

Stack
Laravel · Ionic · Angular

The problem

Small employers track attendance on paper and approve leave over messages. When staff work across sites, nobody knows who is actually where.

Hazrify brings attendance, leave and location into one app for employers and their teams.

How it was built

  1. 01

    Many employers, one platform

    A multi-tenant Laravel backend, with separate dashboards for employers and employees.

  2. 02

    One app for every phone

    An Ionic and Angular app, packaged with Capacitor for iOS and Android.

  3. 03

    Attendance with context

    Check-ins with location monitoring, and leave requests that follow an approval workflow.
