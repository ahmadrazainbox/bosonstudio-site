Who we are

Boson Studio is a software studio based near Askari Bank, Helan Chungi, Phalia, Mandi Bahauddin, Pakistan. We run this website and are responsible for the information it collects. Questions about this notice go to contact@bosonstudio.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the options you tick and your message. The form prepares a draft in your email app. These details reach contact@bosonstudio.com only when you send that email. The website does not keep them in a database.
  • Delivery and security. Our hosting and network providers, including GitHub Pages, process IP addresses and request details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and may keep standard server logs.
  • Direct contact. Anything you include when you email or call us.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, discuss your project, and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising tools. It stores up to two small values in your browser's local storage, which never leave your device. You can review or clear them at any time from the privacy panel:

Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell people from automated traffic. It is not used to track you across sites.

Other services

Some links take you to other services, such as Cal.com and Zcal for booking a call, Google Maps for directions, and our team members' personal websites. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to respond and for our ordinary business records, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop using it. Email contact@bosonstudio.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you live in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
