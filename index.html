04How a project runs

Collision, field, light.

The brand is named after the Higgs boson, and our projects follow the same three steps as the physics. They happen in order.

  1. 01 · Collision

    Understand the problem

    A hard problem meets a team that has seen one like it. We start with your goals, your users and the work the software has to carry, and ask the question the brief skipped.

  2. 02 · Field

    Build the foundations

    Architecture, data and integrations first: the structure nobody sees but everything depends on. We build in clear stages, with working software to review along the way.

  3. 03 · Light

    Launch and keep it running

    The visible result of invisible work. We check the details, document the system, and agree on what happens after launch, so nothing goes down and nothing waits.
